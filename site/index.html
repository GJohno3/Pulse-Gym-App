Install iPhone 17 App & Backup

Deploy to Netlify Drop or backup your workout logs

iPhone 17 Ready

1. Download Standalone App File (index.html)

Downloads this entire app as index.html with your custom exercises, logged weights, and Assault Bike history permanently baked into its starting code. On iPhone, choose Save to Files.

2. iPhone 17 Files → Netlify Steps

  1. In Files, put index.html in your PulseGym folder, next to sw.js, manifest.webmanifest & the icon PNGs (replace the old one).
  2. Long-press the PulseGym folder → tap Compress to create PulseGym.zip.
  3. Updating? In Netlify open your existing site → Deploys → upload the zip. Keeping the same URL keeps the workouts saved on this phone.
  4. New site? Upload at app.netlify.com/drop, open the link in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
